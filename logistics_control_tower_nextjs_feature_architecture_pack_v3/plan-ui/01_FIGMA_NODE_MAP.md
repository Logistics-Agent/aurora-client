## Mandatory frame coverage

- S01–S35, all documented Figma states, and S06 Create Shipment steps 1–6.
- Customer Portal: Overview, My Shipments, Detail, Tracking, Documents, Quotes, Invoices, Assistant, Notifications; plus mobile list/detail/tracking/documents/invoice/notifications/assistant.
- Global states: loading, empty, error, partial failure, disconnected/reconnecting, GPS stale/offline, AI queued/processing/completed/needs-review/failed.
- Confirmation dialogs: Cancel Shipment, Delete Draft, Accept AI Route, Approve/Reject OCR, Approve Negotiation Offer, Record Payment, Mark Compliance Resolved.
